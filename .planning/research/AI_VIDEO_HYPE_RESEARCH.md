# AI Video Idea Research: Hype-First Revision

**Date:** 2026-04-30  
**Why this exists:** The first research pass over-optimized for business utility and under-optimized for YouTube attention. This revision uses a creator-first filter.

## Better Filter For This Channel

A good idea needs all four:

1. **One-sentence hook:** a viewer understands the video instantly.
2. **AI does real work:** collects data, makes choices, creates assets, or automates a workflow.
3. **Visible result:** something appears on screen: leads, redesigns, ads, posts, reports, money math.
4. **Viewer fantasy:** "I want this for myself/my business."

Weak idea:

> AI analyzes reviews and makes a report.

Strong idea:

> I built an AI employee that finds businesses with angry customers and creates offers to steal demand.

## Market Direction

The market is moving from "AI tools" to "AI agents/operators": systems that research, analyze, write, decide, and execute workflows. Current coverage repeatedly points at agents for SMBs, solopreneurs, content operations, lead generation, and business automation. That means the better video angle is not "AI dashboard"; it is "AI employee does a job."

Useful market signals:

- SMBs are adopting AI for concrete business outcomes, especially lead generation, analytics, and agentic workflows.
- Creator/business YouTube still rewards simple money/workflow hooks: business systems, AI productivity, software tutorials, side-hustle playbooks.
- Generic "AI audit" and "AI summary" products feel boring unless tied to a visible action.

## Top Ideas Reframed Properly

### 1. AI Sales Intern For Local Businesses

**Better hook:** I built an AI employee that finds businesses I can sell to.

**What it does:**
1. User picks a service: websites, bots, ads, design, automation.
2. AI searches local businesses in a city.
3. It finds weak signals:
   - bad reviews;
   - no website;
   - old website;
   - slow site;
   - weak Instagram/Telegram;
   - no online booking;
   - repeated customer complaints.
4. It creates:
   - lead table;
   - personalized pain summary;
   - custom offer;
   - cold email/message draft;
   - optional before/after website or bot concept.

**Why people watch:** It feels like an AI sales employee that can make money.

**Video title options:**
- "I Built an AI Employee That Finds Clients For Me"
- "AI Found 50 Businesses I Could Sell Websites To"
- "I Let AI Find Clients For My Freelance Business"

**MVP:** Start with one city and one service. Example: "Find dental clinics in Krasnoyarsk with bad reviews/no booking and pitch a Telegram booking bot."

### 2. AI Freelancer That Applies To Jobs

**Better hook:** I built an AI freelancer that finds gigs and writes proposals while I sleep.

**What it does:**
1. Parses Telegram/freelance job sources.
2. Filters jobs that match the freelancer profile.
3. Scores fit, budget, urgency, risk.
4. Creates:
   - proposal draft;
   - price estimate;
   - timeline;
   - clarifying questions;
   - "red flags" warning.

**Important boundary:** It should draft replies, not spam-send them automatically.

**Why people watch:** Direct money fantasy. This also connects perfectly to the bot already built.

**Video title options:**
- "I Built an AI Freelancer That Finds Work For Me"
- "This AI Reads Freelance Jobs and Writes My Proposals"
- "Can AI Get Me Telegram Bot Clients?"

**MVP:** Use the current Telegram lead bot as the input layer, then add AI proposal generation.

### 3. AI Competitor Stealer From Reviews

**Better hook:** I built AI that reads bad reviews and tells a business how to steal customers.

**What it does:**
1. Takes reviews from competitors.
2. Finds repeated complaints.
3. Converts each complaint into:
   - service improvement;
   - offer angle;
   - ad headline;
   - landing section;
   - objection answer.

**Why this is better than the original framing:** "Review analysis" is boring. "Steal customers from angry reviews" is a clickable story.

**Video title options:**
- "AI Found What Customers Hate About Competitors"
- "I Used AI To Turn Bad Reviews Into Ads"
- "AI Read Competitor Reviews and Built a Better Offer"

**MVP:** No full platform needed. Use exported/copied reviews for the video, then later add parsing.

### 4. AI Rebuilds A Bad Local Business Website

**Better hook:** I gave AI an ugly business website and it rebuilt it to sell better.

**What it does:**
1. Finds or accepts a weak website.
2. Screenshots the page.
3. Extracts business type, offer, CTA, trust signals.
4. Compares against competitor examples.
5. Generates:
   - new headline;
   - page structure;
   - copy;
   - visual direction;
   - optional HTML prototype.

**Why people watch:** Before/after is instantly visible.

**Video title options:**
- "AI Rebuilt This Terrible Business Website"
- "I Used AI To Redesign a Local Business in 10 Minutes"
- "AI Roasted and Fixed a Website"

**MVP:** One-page HTML rebuild. No big SaaS needed.

### 5. AI Content Factory From One Video

**Better hook:** I upload one video, AI turns it into a week of content.

**What it does:**
1. Takes one YouTube video/transcript.
2. Extracts best moments and hooks.
3. Creates:
   - Shorts ideas;
   - Telegram posts;
   - newsletter;
   - YouTube description;
   - thumbnail concepts;
   - title variants.

**Why people watch:** Every creator understands the pain.

**Video title options:**
- "I Built an AI Content Factory For My YouTube Channel"
- "One Video In, 20 Posts Out"
- "AI Turned My Video Into a Week of Content"

**MVP:** Use transcript first. Later add video clipping.

## What To Stop Prioritizing

### Stop leading with "audit", "analysis", "dashboard"

These words feel like homework.

Use:
- "AI employee";
- "AI intern";
- "AI finds clients";
- "AI writes proposals";
- "AI steals ideas ethically";
- "AI rebuilds";
- "AI turns X into money/action".

### Stop choosing tiny B2B niches first

Tiny niches may be monetizable, but weak for a broad YouTube video. Start with broad fantasies:
- get clients;
- make content faster;
- beat competitors;
- improve a business;
- make money from freelance work.

Then niche down inside the build.
