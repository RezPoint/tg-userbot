# AI Video Idea Research

**Date:** 2026-04-30  
**Goal:** Find AI product/video ideas where the value is not just prompting ChatGPT, but collecting real-world data, analyzing it, and returning a concrete action plan.

## Decision Filter

A strong idea for this channel should pass most of these:

1. **Real data input:** reviews, posts, ads, sites, screenshots, chats, marketplace listings, CRM exports.
2. **Visible transformation:** messy data becomes a table, report, action plan, content plan, offer, proposal, or design fix list.
3. **Not trivial ChatGPT:** the hard part is data gathering, comparison, scoring, extraction, or grounding with evidence.
4. **Good video demo:** before/after is easy to show on screen.
5. **Useful now:** a freelancer, local business, creator, seller, designer, or agency can use it this week.
6. **MVP-friendly:** can be built as a local script or small web app without enterprise integrations.

## Top 5 Build Briefs

### 1. AI Review Offer Miner

**One-liner:** AI reads competitor reviews and turns their weaknesses into your offers.

**Best demo niche:** local services with emotional reviews: стоматология, барбершоп, доставка еды, клининг, автосервис, частная клиника, салон красоты.

**MVP input options:**
- Phase 1: paste/export reviews manually or CSV.
- Phase 2: parse public review pages where allowed.
- Phase 3: user enters niche/city and app finds competitors.

**Pipeline:**
1. User enters business type + city or competitor URLs.
2. Collect 50-300 reviews.
3. Normalize text and remove duplicates.
4. AI extracts categories: service, price, speed, quality, communication, trust, location, warranty.
5. Cluster pains and praise.
6. Generate actions:
   - fix in operations;
   - offer angle;
   - ad copy;
   - landing section;
   - FAQ objection answer.

**Output sections:**
- "Top 10 pains customers complain about";
- "Competitors' weak spots";
- "What to promise";
- "What not to promise";
- "5 ready ads";
- "7-day implementation checklist".

**Why not just ChatGPT:** ChatGPT does not have the reviews, frequency counts, quotes, competitor split, or source evidence unless the app collects/structures them.

**Video title options:**
- "Я сделал AI, который читает плохие отзывы конкурентов и придумывает офферы"
- "Как украсть спрос у конкурентов с помощью AI и отзывов"
- "AI нашел, за что клиенты ненавидят конкурентов"

**Risks:**
- Scraping terms vary by platform.
- Google/Yandex/2GIS data access can be brittle.
- Safer first video: user exports/copies reviews, then app analyzes.

### 2. AI Telegram Competitor Auditor

**One-liner:** AI studies Telegram competitors and tells you what to post and sell.

**Best demo niche:** Telegram bot developers, AI automation, инфобизнес, маркетинг, психология, нутрициология, WB/Ozon sellers.

**Pipeline:**
1. User enters niche.
2. Discover public channels via search/directories/seed list.
3. Parse recent posts with views, reactions, forwards, comments if available.
4. Calculate performance relative to channel baseline.
5. AI extracts:
   - themes;
   - offers;
   - hooks;
   - CTA;
   - post format;
   - proof patterns;
   - controversy/engagement triggers.
6. Generate content plan and post drafts.

**Output sections:**
- "What works in this niche";
- "Repeated offers";
- "Best hooks";
- "Top 20 post ideas";
- "14-day content plan";
- "5 ready posts in your angle".

**Why not just ChatGPT:** needs channel discovery, post parsing, engagement baselines, and competitor evidence.

**Video title options:**
- "Я сделал AI, который шпионит за Telegram-конкурентами"
- "AI разобрал конкурентов в Telegram и написал мне контент-план"

**Risks:**
- Telegram search/discovery is imperfect.
- Comments are available only when discussion groups are accessible.
- Existing analytics tools cover metrics; our value must be "what to do next".

### 3. AI Marketplace Review Miner

**One-liner:** AI reads competitor product reviews and tells sellers how to improve the product card, product, and ads.

**Best demo niche:** cosmetics, supplements, household goods, clothing sizing, electronics accessories, pet products.

**Pipeline:**
1. User gives product URL(s).
2. Collect reviews and listing metadata.
3. AI extracts complaints, praise, buyer vocabulary, missing details, sizing/packaging/delivery issues.
4. Generate:
   - listing title rewrite;
   - bullet points;
   - FAQ;
   - image shot list;
   - ad hooks;
   - product improvement checklist.

**Why not just ChatGPT:** review evidence and frequency counts matter; seller needs grounded changes.

**Video title options:**
- "AI прочитал отзывы конкурентов на маркетплейсе и переписал карточку товара"
- "Как AI нашел, почему товар конкурента ругают, и сделал оффер лучше"

**Risks:** marketplace scraping and anti-bot systems. Use exported reviews or public pages first.

### 4. AI Client Brief -> Scope Shield

**One-liner:** AI turns messy client chats into a proposal and catches scope creep before it becomes unpaid work.

**Pipeline:**
1. User pastes Telegram/WhatsApp/client chat.
2. AI extracts goals, deliverables, assumptions, unknowns, risks, timeline.
3. Generates:
   - clarifying questions;
   - proposal;
   - price options;
   - scope boundaries;
   - "future change order" clauses.
4. Later: user pastes new client message; AI says whether it is in-scope or paid extra.

**Why not just ChatGPT:** if we add structured templates, memory of agreed scope, and change-order detection, it becomes workflow software, not just a prompt.

**Video title options:**
- "Я сделал AI, который защищает фрилансера от бесплатных доработок"
- "AI превратил переписку с клиентом в ТЗ и коммерческое предложение"

**Risks:** crowded proposal-generator market. The scope-creep angle is the differentiator.

### 5. AI Ad Swipe Deconstructor

**One-liner:** AI reads competitor ads and turns them into reusable hooks, angles, scripts, and creative briefs.

**Pipeline:**
1. User uploads ad screenshots/video links or public ad library results.
2. AI extracts format, hook, pain, promise, proof, CTA, visual pattern.
3. Clusters repeated angles.
4. Generates:
   - swipe file;
   - 10 hooks;
   - 5 scripts;
   - creative brief for designer/video editor;
   - "what to test first".

**Why not just ChatGPT:** requires real ad examples and visual decomposition.

**Video title options:**
- "AI разобрал рекламу конкурентов и написал мои креативы"
- "Как я сделал мини-SpyFu для рекламных креативов"

**Risks:** public ad access varies by platform; start with screenshots/links.

## Recommended Sequence For Videos

### Video 1: AI Review Offer Miner

Why first:
- strongest "business value" story;
- easy to understand in 10 seconds;
- visible before/after;
- can be MVP without fragile scraping;
- useful to local businesses and agencies.

MVP scope:
- CSV/paste reviews first;
- optional competitor search later;
- output markdown/CSV report.

### Video 2: AI Telegram Competitor Auditor

Why second:
- builds on Telegram parsing experience;
- good fit for your current audience and previous bot video;
- more technically interesting.

MVP scope:
- seed channel list first;
- then add channel discovery;
- parse public posts and compute relative engagement.

### Video 3: AI Marketplace Review Miner

Why third:
- strong e-commerce/WB/Ozon angle;
- practical and visual;
- can reuse review clustering logic from Video 1.

### Video 4: AI Client Brief -> Scope Shield

Why fourth:
- perfect for freelancers;
- no scraping headaches;
- very useful, but less "wow" than competitor data.

## Next Research Questions

Before building each idea, answer:

1. What exact data source is reliable enough for a demo?
2. Can we legally/ethically collect it, or should we start with user-provided exports?
3. What output would make a viewer say "I can use this today"?
4. Which existing tools are closest?
5. What is the one narrow wedge that makes our version not generic?

## Final Recommendation

Build and film these in this order:

1. **AI Review Offer Miner** — best mix of business usefulness, story, and MVP feasibility.
2. **AI Telegram Competitor Auditor** — strongest continuity with the Telegram parser work.
3. **AI Marketplace Review Miner** — strong spin-off using the same review analysis engine.
4. **AI Client Brief -> Scope Shield** — practical freelancer tool with less scraping risk.
