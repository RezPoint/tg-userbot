# Video Product Ideas

## 1. AI Review Offer Miner

**Hook:** AI reads competitors' bad reviews and turns them into offers for your business.

**Input:** niche, city, competitor names/URLs, or exported reviews from 2GIS/Yandex/Google.

**MVP Flow:**
1. Find or ingest 50-100 competitor reviews.
2. Group pain points, praise, unmet expectations, and repeated complaints.
3. Extract customer quotes as proof.
4. Generate business actions, offer ideas, ad copy, FAQ, and landing-page blocks.

**Output:**
- pain table with frequency and quotes;
- "what competitors do badly";
- offer ideas;
- ready ad texts;
- checklist of service/product improvements.

**Video Angle:** "I built an AI that reads competitors' reviews and tells a business how to steal demand ethically."

## 2. AI Telegram Competitor Auditor

**Hook:** AI analyzes Telegram competitors and tells you what to post, what offers repeat, and which formats work.

**Input:** niche, own channel, or competitor channel list.

**MVP Flow:**
1. Find 10-20 relevant Telegram channels.
2. Parse recent posts, views, reactions, and available comments.
3. Detect high-performing themes, hooks, CTAs, and repeated offers.
4. Generate content strategy and concrete posts.

**Output:**
- top content themes;
- repeated offers and CTAs;
- post formats that overperform;
- 14/30-day content plan;
- ready post drafts.

**Video Angle:** "I built an AI that studies Telegram competitors and writes my content plan."
